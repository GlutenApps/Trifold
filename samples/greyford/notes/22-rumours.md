Roll a d6 (open the dice pool from the live strip) when someone in the Wayside Lamp is talking. Entries marked _false_ are wrong, but believed.

| d6  | Rumour                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | "The bandits aren't from round here. Their captain talks like a city sergeant." _(True: Mara served in a garrison.)_     |
| 2   | "Old Hesper's dead, and the lantern died with her." _(False: she's alive in the shrine's storehouse.)_                   |
| 3   | "There was a priest in town a fortnight back, asking about the shrine's records. Paid in old silver." _(True: Corvin.)_  |
| 4   | "Tobin Marsh went up for a look. He's a good lad. He'll be fine." _(Half true: alive, not fine.)_                        |
| 5   | "Something's been walking in the shrine at night. The bell rings with nobody near it." _(True.)_                         |
| 6   | "The lantern going out means the Grey will flood. It did last time." _(True, though nobody believes it.)_               |
