_Scene: Grey Gorge._ The whole shrine is on one map. The party comes in at the south-west corner, where the path from Greyford reaches the gorge.

The path climbs through oak woods and drops into the gorge. The river is loud and higher than it should be. Ahead, two red-railed bridges meet on a rock with an enormous seated statue, and beyond them the shrine's halls and wind-wheels step up the cliffs. There's no light anywhere except a cookfire on the statue's rock.

The grid is 5 ft. The river is fast and waist-deep: difficult terrain, and a creature that ends its turn in it must succeed on a DC 10 Strength save or be pushed 10 ft downstream (east).

## The bridges

Mara Venn's crew have held the gorge mouth for a week on Corvin's silver.

- **Mara** sits by the fire at the foot of the statue (**G1**).
- **The scouts** are in the bell house (**G4**) with bows, watching the bridges across the water.
- **Two bandits** guard the south bridge (**G2**) and **two** the north bridge's red gate (**G3**).
- **Three wolves**, kept half-starved, lie around the statue rock. They wake at any noise.

If Gorm warned her, she's ready: the party is surprised at the end of the path unless someone beats a DC 14 Perception check. Add the extra wolf. If Gorm is a prisoner, or the party comes quietly, a DC 13 Stealth check gets them to the south bridge unseen.

Mara would rather be paid than fight: "Gorge is closed. Ten gold a head to go back down and forget it." She can't be bribed past the bridges, because the priest pays better. She can be talked into a duel, or intimidated by a party that has just taken Gorm apart (DC 16).

Start _Holding the bridges_ from the scene.

## Mara runs

When Mara drops to half her HP or below (26 HP), she runs across the north bridge and east into the shrine, to Corvin. Her bandits give up a round later. **Don't chase this down.** End the fight with her still alive. Trifold keeps her HP, and she'll turn up in the last fight still hurt. If the party kills or catches her instead, she won't be there, and the last fight is easier for it.

## After the fight

The bandits' packs by the fire hold a week of Greyford's "road money": 60 gp of tarnished silver stamped with a lantern, the shrine's mark. The statue rock is a good place for a short rest.

Captured bandits know: "The priest's been in the big shrine hall a week. He took the lantern down off the gate. We hear him talking in there. Nobody answers."
