The text of the handout image (the *Hesper's letter* scene). Read it aloud too, for anyone who can't see the screen.

> Wren,
>
> I am writing because I am old and you were the only student I had who listened. Someone has been asking after the chapel records: a man with a priest's manners and none of a priest's sense. I sent him away. He came back at night.
>
> If you are reading this and I have not written again, come to Greyford. Come quickly. Keep it lit. Keep it high.
>
> — H. Q.
