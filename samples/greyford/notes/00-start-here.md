This is a sample campaign: a complete one-session adventure for four level-3 characters, set up so you can see what every part of Trifold does. Run it as written, pull it apart, or delete it when you're done. Deleting it never touches your other campaigns, and **Settings › Sample campaign** brings it back.

## A five-minute tour

**Campaign tab: notes, NPCs and the party.** You're reading a note now. The tree on the left holds folders: _Adventure_ is the script for the night, _World_ is background you can improvise from, and _Sessions_ holds prep and a play log. Search finds any phrase in any note. The party is four ready-made characters you can hand to players or replace with their own. The NPCs have stat blocks, so they can go straight into a fight.

**Compendium tab.** Every monster in these fights is an SRD creature. Open the Compendium and search "bandit captain" to see Mara Venn's stat block.

**Encounters tab.** Four fights are already built. Open one to see its difficulty against the party's budget. Starting a fight turns this tab into the combat tracker.

**Scenes, in the dock on the right.** This is what your players see. Click a scene to select it, and send it to the TV to show it. Try the title card first, then the village map. The TV tab shows the selected scene with every control for the screen: grid, camera, overlays, blackout and handouts.

**Live strip, along the bottom.** What's on the TV right now, the round and turn, music, and dice. It only mirrors controls that also live in their own tools.

## Try a fight without players

1. Open the Scenes dock and select **The Wayside Lamp**.
2. In the TV tab, click the map to drop the party by the fire pit.
3. The bandits are already on the map, hidden from the players. Start combat from the scene, and the tracker rolls initiative for each group.
4. Roll an attack from a stat block, apply some damage, add a condition, and step through a few turns.
5. End the fight. Gorm Tallow is an NPC, so whatever HP he has left is kept for next time.

## Things to notice later

- **Maps with more than one look.** The Wayside Lamp has **Night** and **Day** backgrounds, and Grey Gorge has **Low water** and **Flood**. Switch them from the map's backgrounds menu while the map is on the TV; tokens and the grid stay put.
- **Big maps and the player camera.** Grey Gorge is too big to show whole at a readable size. Use the player camera to frame the part of it the party is in, or let it follow the active combatant.
- **Mara Venn** can flee the bridges fight hurt. If she does, add her to the last fight with _Add to fight_, and she arrives with the HP she left with.
- **The last fight isn't on the map yet.** When the party reaches the shrine hall, use _Place encounter…_ on the gorge map, position the creatures while they're hidden, then start it.
- **Hesper Quill** joins the last fight on the party's side if they free her first.
- **Music** isn't included. Each scene's notes suggest a mood, so link your own playlists to the scenes.
- **Layouts** (top bar) switch the console between Prep, Table and Combat arrangements.
