## G6. The keepers' rooms (the first fight)

Back on the south bank, west of the shrine: a kitchen with shelves, tables and a great iron stove, and next to it a library, its floor covered in books and old bones. The keepers were buried under these floors for two hundred years.

When anyone steps into either room, the dead get up. Start _The keepers' rooms_ from the Encounters tab: its creatures are already on the map, hidden. Skeletons pull themselves out of the library floor, zombies shamble out of the kitchen, and rats pour from under the stove. **The ghoul** stays in the library's far corner and only attacks once someone is down or alone.

In the library, a DC 12 Investigation check finds a sealed lead tube under a loose board: a _spell scroll of Lesser Restoration_ and a _potion of healing_, the keepers' old emergency kit. Hesper knows it's there.

## G8. The storehouse

Pillars, shelves of jars, rolled mats. **Hesper Quill** and **Tobin Marsh** are tied to a pillar. Both are weak with thirst but awake. Hesper is furious, mostly at herself.

- She tells the party everything about Corvin, his sister, and what the flame does off its gate.
- The flame has to go back up on the gate rock (**G10**), lit, before the river takes the shrine. She knows the words to relight it.
- She insists on coming. She can't be talked out of it. In the last fight, add her as an **ally** in the tracker.
- Tobin should be sent back to Greyford. He goes if anyone asks kindly.

## G13. The shrine hall (the last fight)

Six stone keepers sit along the walls. Between the four pillars, on the round dais, the still flame burns in a bowl of black iron, much too bright, and the shadows on the walls don't match the people casting them. **Brother Corvin** kneels at the dais, talking softly to nobody.

When the party reaches the doors, use **Place encounter…** on the gorge map to drop _The still flame_ into the shrine hall. Corvin is at the dais, the cultists between the pillars, and the shadows by the seated statues. Position everything while it's still hidden, then start the fight.

- Corvin pleads first. "She's almost here. Can't you hear her?" A character who listens hears a woman's voice, but it's saying their own name. Give a DC 14 Insight check to realise the flame is imitating whoever is nearest.
- **The flood.** At the start of round 2, the river comes up the gorge in one roar. **Switch the map to Flood.** The riverbed is now white water: anyone outside a building or off a bridge must succeed on a DC 13 Strength save at the end of each turn or be pushed 15 ft downstream and take 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
- **If Mara escaped the bridges**, she's here already, hiding behind a statue, and joins in round 2 when she sees which way it's going. Add her with _Add to fight_ as an enemy, unless the party is clearly winning and offers her a way out (DC 14 Persuasion turns her neutral, and she leaves).
- **The flame:** while it burns on the dais, the shadows regain 5 HP at the start of each of their turns. A character can grab the bowl as an action (DC 12 Strength check, 1d6 cold damage) and carry it. The shadows then lose their regeneration and spend their turns trying to take it back.
- Corvin fights until he drops or the flame leaves the hall. Then he sits down on the dais steps and weeps, and doesn't resist.

## Carrying the flame home

From the shrine hall, the gate rock (**G10**) is a short run west along the south bank and up the spiral stair: easy at low water, a real danger in the flood. Let each character describe how they get there, and call for the Strength saves above. Then go to _Ending and rewards_.
