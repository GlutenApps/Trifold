## Relighting the lantern

At the top of the gate rock's spiral stair, the lantern housing hangs from the gate's crossbeam, its little glass door open and cold. Set the flame in it and Hesper, or Wren with Hesper's words, speaks the relighting. Ask for a DC 10 Religion check, or skip the check if Hesper is alive: it's only a question of how it looks. The flame steadies and goes pale, and its light falls the length of the gorge.

The river drops as if a plug had been pulled. **Switch the map back to Low water.** In the keepers' rooms, everything that was moving lies down at once.

Show the last title card, **The lantern burns again**.

## If things went badly

- **Hesper died:** the flame can still be relit, but nobody keeps it now. Greyford asks the party, or Wren, to stay. That's a good hook for a second session.
- **The party retreats:** the river breaks out of the gorge that night and the dead walk with it. Greyford sees the first of them at dawn. Run the next session as a defence of the village, using the blank-grid scene and the tavern map.

## What happens to Corvin

He can be handed to the reeve, sent to his old order, or let go. Hesper asks for him to be spared: "He was a good student. That's the problem." Mara, if she lived, is long gone.

## Rewards

- The party reaches **level 4**.
- 60 gp in shrine silver from the bandits' packs, plus Mara's purse (25 gp) if she was caught.
- From the library: a _spell scroll of Lesser Restoration_ and a _potion of healing_.
- From Oswin: free board at the Wayside Lamp for as long as they want it.
- From Hesper, if she lives: a small lantern charm for each character. It's no use in a fight, but its light is welcome in any home in the valley, and that is worth more around here.
