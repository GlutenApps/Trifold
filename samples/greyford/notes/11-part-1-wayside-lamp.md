_Scenes: The Lantern at Greyford (title), Greyford, Hesper's letter, The Wayside Lamp._

## Opening

Show the title card, then the village map. Read or paraphrase:

> Rain has followed you down the east road all afternoon. Greyford is a few dozen blue-slate roofs at a crossroads, a timber yard, a green with one enormous oak, and an inn with a lamp in every window. To the north-east, past the woods, you can hear the river Grey in its gorge. Every traveller on this road knows the light that hangs in that gorge, the one that never goes out.
>
> Tonight there's no light over the trees.

Each character has a reason to be here (see their cards). Wren carries Hesper's letter; send it to the TV as a handout when she reads it.

About a dozen locals sit close to the fire pit, talking low. Oswin Barrow hurries over with dry towels and a lot of questions. What the room knows (one rumour per conversation, or roll on _Rumours_ in World):

- The lantern in the gorge went out three nights ago. Nobody has seen Hesper since.
- Tobin Marsh went up the gorge two nights ago to look. Pell, his father, sits alone by the fire.
- Mara Venn's bandits have held the bridges at the mouth of the gorge for a week. Anyone who tries them gets turned back, or worse.
- The river's been rising, and there was no rain to speak of until today.

## The shakedown

Once the party has had a drink and a rumour or two, the front door (**I1**) bangs open. **Gorm Tallow** and three toughs come in out of the rain. Two bandits are already at the north long table, and two more on the bar stools get up to join him. They were watching.

Gorm wants Oswin's "road money", twelve silver, and he wants to be seen getting it. If the party steps in, he sizes them up and pushes. Let the players choose:

- **Pay or talk him down.** A DC 15 Persuasion or Intimidation check sends him off with a sneer, and he warns Mara the party is coming. The bridges then get one extra wolf.
- **Fight.** Start _Trouble at the Wayside Lamp_ from the scene. The locals scatter into the back hall. Gorm gives up when he's bloodied and the toughs are down.

## After

If Gorm is captured, he'll trade what he knows for his freedom: Mara holds the bridges at the gorge mouth, and "the priest" pays her in silver stamped with the shrine's lantern.

Oswin gives the party rooms for free. In the morning (switch the map to **Day**), Pell lends them his storm lantern and asks them, quietly, to bring his son home.
