## Characters

- **Brannoc**: owes Oswin money. Wants to be the one who fixes things.
- **Wren**: Hesper's old student. Wants Hesper alive.
- **Tamsin**: Tobin's cousin. Wants Tobin home.
- **Ilyra**: came to study the still flame. Wants to see it up close. Careful what you wish for.

## Strong start

Rain, no light over the trees, and then the door of the Wayside Lamp banging open on Gorm Tallow.

## Scenes

- The shakedown at the inn.
- Fighting across the bridges at the gorge mouth.
- The keepers' rooms waking up.
- Corvin at the flame, and the river coming up the gorge.
- Climbing the gate rock in the flood to hang the lantern back up.

## Secrets and clues

1. The lantern went out three nights ago.
2. Hesper is alive, held in the shrine's storehouse.
3. Tobin went up the gorge two nights ago.
4. Mara's bandits are paid in shrine silver.
5. "The priest" took the lantern down off its gate.
6. Corvin's sister Lissa drowned in the Grey's flood ten years ago.
7. Off its gate, the flame raises the river and wakes the dead.
8. The keepers left an emergency kit under the library floor.
9. The river will keep rising until the flame is back on the gate.
10. Only Hesper knows the words to relight the flame.

## Locations

- **The Wayside Lamp**: every lamp lit, as if that would help.
- **The statue rock**: a bandit cookfire in the lap of a giant stone keeper.
- **The keepers' rooms**: books and bones all over the library floor.
- **The shrine hall**: shadows that don't match their owners.
- **The gate rock**: an empty lantern housing swinging in the wind.
