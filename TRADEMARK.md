# The Trifold name and logo

Trifold's code is free software under the GNU General Public License (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
The licence covers the code, not the name. **"Trifold" and the Trifold logo** (the files in
`docs/brand/` and `apps/desktop/build/`) belong to Ian duPont and are not licensed under the GPL.

## What you can do without asking

- Say what is true: that your project is based on Trifold, works with Trifold, or is a fork of it.
- Use the name to refer to Trifold itself, in articles, videos, reviews and teaching.
- Build, run and share unchanged copies of official releases under their own name.

## What needs permission

- Calling a changed version, a fork or another product "Trifold", or a name that could be
  mistaken for it.
- Using the logo, or something like it, for a changed version or another product.
- Suggesting that a project, service or product is official or endorsed when it is not.

A fork is welcome: give it its own name and icon, and say that it is based on Trifold.

To ask about anything else, open an issue on the project's repository.
