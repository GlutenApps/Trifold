Shell anatomy · 1600 × 900
1Top bar, 34 px. The app's header and, since the window is frameless, its title bar: the mark opens the Library; the breadcrumb names the open campaign and drops the switcher; then the current layout, Hotkeys, Settings (in the Library), the dock toggle and the window controls. Nothing here is table state.
2Stage tab strip, 34 px. One 28 px tab per open major: Campaign, Compendium, Encounters, Combat, Map. All five are present by default; close what you don't need, + brings it back or moves it from the other group. Accent top line: active. Pip: live. Ring: unsaved changes. ⧉ splits right, ⤢ maximizes.
3Stage group. Holds the active major. Switching never resets a search, a selection, an open map or an encounter. Majors never appear in the dock.
4Split divider, 8 px. Drag to resize. Two groups is the ceiling: enough for combat beside the map, not enough to rebuild the old panel wall.
5Contextual toolbar. A major keeps its tools inside itself: the map editor's stand in a 36 px strip so the canvas stays wide at 600 px.
6Dock tab strip. Six toggles for the minors: Dice, TV controls, Music, Scenes, Hotkeys, Layouts. Several can be open at once. Pip: live. ⇥ collapses to icons. Minors never appear on the stage.
7Dock sections. Open minors stack as collapsible sections; the last open one takes what is left and scrolls inside. The dock is 400 wide, 300 narrow, 44 as icons, or hidden. Icons only or hidden, a tool opens as a flyout; Escape closes it.
8Live strip, 42 px. Table state only: what is on the TV or Blackout, player window, round and turn, music and mute, the shared dice pool and last total. Clicking a section opens its dock tool or focuses the Combat tab.
9One tap from anywhere. Blackout, player window, undo scene, previous and next turn, panic mute and Roll all live on the strip, each with its hotkey.
